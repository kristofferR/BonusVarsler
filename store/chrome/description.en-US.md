Bonus programs such as Trumf, SAS EuroBonus, DNB, OBOS and Spenn give you cashback, discounts or points at hundreds of online stores, but only when you start your shopping trip from their websites. This unofficial extension displays a notification when you visit a store that participates in one of the programs, so you don't miss out on your bonus.

✓ Supports eleven bonus programs, and you choose which ones you want notifications for
✓ Adblocker check (some programs require adblockers to be turned off)
✓ Reminder when you are on the bonus portal itself
✓ Lightning-fast and resource-friendly, you won't notice it's running
✓ Clean design with light and dark mode
✓ Drag the notification to your preferred corner, or minimize it
✓ Hide the notification on sites where you don't want it
✓ Respects your privacy, no tracking. A member ID you enter is stored only locally and is sent only to that bonus program when you click through to it yourself.

This is an independent hobby project with no affiliation to the bonus programs or the stores.

All supported bonus programs and open source: https://github.com/kristofferR/BonusVarsler
